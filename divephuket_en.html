⚠️ Safety – King Cruiser (32m)

Depth: 32–35m = at AOWD limit! Conservative depth limit for companions: Max. 28–30m instead of 35m.

Challenges:

  • Depth: 32–35m = at AOWD limit (max. 30m without Deep Specialty)
  • Current: Moderate to strong (tide-dependent)
  • Structure: Wreck is unstable – no penetration!
  • Air consumption: Faster with current + depth

Safety measures for AOWD companions:

  • Conservative depth limit: Max. 28–30m instead of 35m
  • Swim around wreck externally – NO penetration!
  • Current management: Always along the wreck, follow guide, DSMB ready
  • Air management: Turn-around point at 100 bar (not 50 bar!)
  • Buddy system: Team of three or 2+1 (you with one, third with guide)
  • Computer always in view: Monitor no-decompression limit

For you (Rescue Diver, Nitrox):

  • Use Nitrox 32%: 32m with Nitrox = longer bottom time (45–50 min instead of 25 min on air)
  • Leadership role: Lead in current, demonstrate proper finning technique
  • Rescue awareness: Know emergency equipment, DAN number saved

After the dive: Drink plenty of water, light meal, go to bed early. Preparation for Day 2 (Racha Noi = challenging!)

General Safety Rules

Before every dive:

  • Buddy check: BCD, valves, regulator, air pressure (200 bar+), computer
  • Listen carefully to briefing: Currents, depth limits, emergency procedures
  • Equipment check: Computer working, DSMB present
  • Health check: Well rested? Hydrated? No cold?

During the dive:

  • Buddy system: Always within sight (max. 2–3m)
  • Monitor computer: No-decompression limit, ascent <9m/min
  • Air management: Turn around at 100 bar
  • Buoyancy: Stay neutral, avoid corals
  • Safety stop: 3 min at 5m (always!)

After the dive:

  • Rehydration: 1–2 liters of water
  • Log your dive
  • No intense activity
  • Surface interval: Min. 1h, better 1.5–2h

24h before return flight:

  • No diving! (Better 48h as in Plan A)
  • No snorkeling deeper than 3m either
  • If symptoms occur: Contact decompression chamber immediately

🎒 5. Optimal Preparation

📋 Documents Essential

  • PADI E-Cards loaded on smartphone (App: "PADI", free iOS/Android)
  • Physical certificates as backup
  • Logbooks (PADI App or physical)
  • Passport
  • Dive insurance card (DAN/Aquamed)
  • Dive medical certificate (if available)
  • All documents as digital copies in cloud storage!

💊 Medical Preparation Essential

Dive Medical Examination

  • When: If >1 year ago or health changes
  • What: Get dive fitness certificate issued

Dive Insurance (ESSENTIAL!)

  • DAN Europe: www.daneurope.org – "Diver" (€39/year) or "Diver Plus" (€89/year)
  • Alternative: Aquamed: www.aquamed.de – often cheaper for short-term insurance (14 days)
  • Covers: Hyperbaric chamber (€10,000+!), medical evacuation, 24/7 emergency hotline, decompression accident treatment, repatriation
  • IMPORTANT: Save emergency number to phone!

Seasickness (important for long boat rides!)

  • Stugeron (Cinnarizine): OTC in Germany, 25mg, 1 tablet 1h before departure, effective 6–8h
  • Alternative: Ginger capsules + Sea-Bands (acupressure)
  • Long rides: Racha Noi 2–2.25h, Phi Phi 1.5–2h, King Cruiser 1.5–2h

Ear Drops

  • EarCalm or Otivent (OTC), in the evening after dives
  • Important: 8–9 dives in 3 days!

Travel Pharmacy

  • Ibuprofen, Paracetamol
  • Antihistamine (jellyfish reactions)
  • Disinfectant, bandages & dressings
  • Reef-safe sunscreen (Stream2Sea, Raw Elements) – mandatory at Marine Sanctuaries!
  • After-sun (after boat rides)

Health Checks Before Departure

  • No cold/ear problems
  • No open wounds
  • Well hydrated
  • Sufficient sleep
