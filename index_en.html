FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got a different question? Drop us a line – we usually answer within one business day.

Does my data stay in the EU?
Yes. With the EU Data Boundary enabled, prompts and responses are processed exclusively in European Microsoft data centers.
Is my content used for model training?
No. M365 Copilot does not use your data to train the foundation models. It stays in your tenant and follows your compliance rules.
How quickly will I see results?
Most features are available immediately after activation. First productivity gains are typically reported by teams within two to four weeks.
Can I extend Copilot with my own data?
Yes – Copilot Studio lets you build agents that tap into your own knowledge sources, APIs or business processes. Low-code, no model training required.
How does licensing work?
Per active user per month. Licenses are bookable monthly or annually, cancellable anytime, and combinable with MCA/EA agreements.
Do I need premium hardware?
No. Copilot runs server-side – a modern browser or up-to-date M365 apps are enough. Copilot+ PCs only add local acceleration on top.
